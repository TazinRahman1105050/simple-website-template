I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Washington State University, Pullman, working under the supervision of Dr. Ananth Kalyanaraman. My research lies at the intersection of bioinformatics/computational biology and high-performance computing. I have 5+ years of experience in developing parallel algorithms and tools for scalable analysis of big data generated from various domains of life sciences. I am a highly organized, collaborative team scientist with exceptional project management skills to keep projects on time. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science & Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET). Highlights: • Developed a parallel, alignment-free sketching-based algorithm that efficiently maps long reads, yielding over 7.13× speedup over the SOTA mappers. Our tool is publicly available at: https://github.com/TazinRahman1105050/JEM-Mapper. • Profound knowledge in Computational Biology, High-performance Computing, Machine Learning, and Data Science • Mentored and held office hours for 120+ students as a teaching assistant at Washington State University. • Participated in many prestigious hackathons, including the WSU Digital Agathon (First Place, 2020) and NVIDIA Agent Toolkit Hackathon. • Actively involved in leadership and service roles: Global Ambassador for the Women in Tech Network and graduate student representative on the WSU EECS Safety Committee. Technical Skills: • Programming: C, C++, R, Pyhton • Parallel Computing: MPI, OpenMP, CUDA • Cloud Platforms: Azure, Amazon AWS
