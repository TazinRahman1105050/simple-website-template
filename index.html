Bio

Tazin Rahman is a PhD candidate conducting research in Computational biology/bioinformatics and High-performance computing, advised by Professor Dr. Ananth Kalyanaraman at Washington State University, Pullman, Washington. In particular, she is interested in developing parallel algorithms for data-intensive problems originating in the areas of computational biology. The resulting tools from her research are scalable for high-throughput data, efficient in practice, and useful for various domains of life sciences. She has published her works in IEEE Transactions on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics, BMC Bioinformatics, and IEEE International Workshop on High Performance Computational Biology. She is passionate about health tech innovation and also has experience in developing AI-driven projects for healthcare. Tazin received her B.S. in computer science and engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, Bangladesh. Tazin is a student member of IEEE, SWE, and AnitaB.org. She is also working as a global ambassador for Women in Tech Network.
